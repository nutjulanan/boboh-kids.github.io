Privacy Policy – Bobo Uploader

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Bobo Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Boboh - โบโบ้ชวนเรียน" to upload the channel's own videos. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

1. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. What the app accesses

  • The owner's own YouTube channel only. After the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app reads the channel name and basic statistics (to confirm the correct channel is connected) and uploads the owner's videos and thumbnails to that channel.
  • The app does not access, collect, or store any data about viewers, subscribers, comments, or any other YouTube users.
  • The app does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

3. How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner's account. It is never sold, shared with, or sent to any third party. Videos are sent only to YouTube.

4. Children

The channel publishes videos made for children, and every upload is marked "made for kids" on YouTube. The app itself does not collect any information from children or from any other person.

5. Removing access and deleting data

The channel owner can disconnect the app at any time from the app's settings page, which deletes the stored token, or revoke access at Google Account → Third-party access. No other data is kept.

6. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
